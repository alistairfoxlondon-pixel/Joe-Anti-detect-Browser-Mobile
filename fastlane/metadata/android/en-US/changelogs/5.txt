1.4.0 — local account, identity templates, proxy failover.

• New: local account gate — first launch creates a device-local email+password account; sign in on cold start; Tools → Lock app re-locks instantly. Local only: PBKDF2 hash in private storage, no server, no telemetry; reset erases only the account.
• New: one-tap identity templates for new profiles (US/UK/DE Windows + Chrome/Edge/Firefox, US iPhone, US/BD Android).
• New: proxy failover — after two consecutive failures a profile is re-pointed at the healthiest watched library proxy (opt-in, Tools).
• Engines tab rebuilt around the official brand marks with single-line pinned builds.
• Static interface copy cut by ~42%; 41 dead strings removed.
• Every page-level guard from 1.3.0 retained: injected-identity verification, proxy-before-load, tracker counter, paste-as-human.
